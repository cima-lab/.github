# Governance

CIMA Lab is an umbrella research lab: hosted projects keep their own PI,
budget, and partners, while the lab provides shared structure, tooling, and
visibility.

## Structure

- **Lab Director:** Dr. Haythem El-Messiry
- **Steering Committee:** Director, Research Area Leads, and the Industry &
  Partnerships Lead — meets monthly on admissions, shared-resource
  allocation, calls, and events
- **Research Area Leads:** one faculty PI per research area, appointed by the
  Steering Committee for two years
- **External Advisory Board:** clinicians, industry, and international
  academics (forming)

## Joining

- **Affiliated faculty:** any CUD faculty member with a relevant project, on
  request to the Director
- **Project admission:** must fit one of the four research areas, have a
  named PI and a student owner, and agree to the lab's shared operating
  standards (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md))
- **Research Area Lead:** appointed by the Steering Committee for a two-year term

## What PIs keep

- Grant ownership, budget authority, and reporting duties
- Authorship and IP per CUD policy; the lab is acknowledged as affiliation
- Freedom to publish under the project's own name with the lab as host

## What the lab provides

- Shared servers, storage, scheduler, and release tooling
- Ethics, data-use, and partnership templates
- Shared students, website, channels, and events
- Grant-writing support and partner introductions

## Decisions and review

Day-to-day decisions rest with the Director; disputes escalate to the Dean's
office. The Steering Committee reviews admissions and resource allocation
monthly. The lab reports annually to the Dean's office and the Advisory Board
on outputs, funding, students, and partnerships. Research areas with no active
project for two years are closed or merged.

## Ethics and conflicts of interest

Human data may only be used under IRB approval and a data-use agreement.
Industry-funded work follows CUD's conflict-of-interest and IP policies.

---

This governance model is subject to change as the lab grows.
